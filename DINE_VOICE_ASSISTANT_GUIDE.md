### **👩 Female Voice (Default):**
- **Pitch:** Higher, melodious tone
- **Rate:** Smooth and clear
- **Style:** Warm and welcoming
- **Perfect for:** Friendly conversations

### **👨 Male Voice:**
- **Pitch:** Lower, confident tone  
- **Rate:** Steady and authoritative
- **Style:** Professional and clear
- **Perfect for:** Formal interactions

### **🎛️ Voice Switching:**
```
"Female voice mein bolo" → Switches to female voice
"Switch to male voice" → Switches to male voice
"Ladki ki awaaz mein baat karo" → Female voice
"Boy voice please" → Male voice
```

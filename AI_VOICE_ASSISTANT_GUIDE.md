## 🎯 **How It Works:**

### **Step 1: Access Voice Assistant**
1. **Login as Customer:** `http://10.151.242.51:3001`
2. **Username:** Any registered customer
3. **Click:** "🎤 Voice Order" tab in navigation

### **Step 2: Start Conversation**
**Say:** `"Hi DineConnect, please take my order"`

**Assistant Response:** `"Hi there! I'm ready to take your order! What would you like to have today?"`

### **Step 3: Place Order Naturally**
**You:** `"Hi DineConnect, please take my order, I wanna have cold coffee"`

**Assistant:** `"Perfect! I heard you want 1 Cold Coffee (₹80). Your total is ₹80. Should I place this order for you? Say 'yes' to confirm or 'no' to change."`

**You:** `"Yes"`

**Assistant:** `"Awesome! Your order has been placed successfully. It will be ready soon. Thank you for using DineConnect!"`

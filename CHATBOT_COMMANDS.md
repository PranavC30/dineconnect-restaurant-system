**Customer:** "What's spicy and popular?"

### 💰 Budget Planning

## 🚀 Pro Tips

### 🎯 Best Practices
- **Be Specific:** "Spicy chicken under ₹200" vs "Show food"
- **Ask Follow-ups:** "Tell me more about that dish"
- **Use Natural Language:** "I'm looking for something sweet"
- **Combine Filters:** "Vegetarian, spicy, under ₹100"

### 🔥 Power User Commands
- "Chef's signature biryani details"
- "Combo for 4 people under ₹800"
- "Quick vegetarian meal with drink"
- "Top 3 desserts with ratings"
- "Gluten-free main course options"
